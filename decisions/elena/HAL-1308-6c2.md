# Decision: the failed banners for a missing, slow or tool-less local model

- Approval: https://paperclip.halonic.com/HAL/issues/HAL-1308 (microcopy 15.3 and failure-states F6-F8 rows for `model-missing`, `model-timeout` and `model-no-tools`, approved with the fifth local-model case)
- Kind: user-visible

The run page's failed banner gets texts for `model-missing`, `model-timeout` and `model-no-tools`, with
**Copy command** and **Open Models** before **Run again**. The requirement `runs.run-view.failure-banners`
gains three rows and loses its "no Open Models yet" limit. Nothing else in it changes, and no test was
weakened.
