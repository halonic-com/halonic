On Windows, signing in to Paperclip, Google or a plugin now opens the sign-in page in a small window of your own browser, centred on Halonic, when your default browser is Chrome, Edge or Brave. Your saved passwords and logins are there as usual. Other links still open in your browser as before, and with another default browser, such as Firefox, sign-in pages open as before too.
