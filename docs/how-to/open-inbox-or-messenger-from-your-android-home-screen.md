# Open Inbox or Messenger from your Android home screen

On Android, you can jump to **Inbox** or **Messenger** from the Halonic icon,
or put either one on your home screen as its own icon.

## Long-press the icon

Long-press the Halonic icon. The menu shows **Inbox** and **Messenger**. Tap one
and the app opens on that tab, with the tab bar showing.

If the app is already open, it switches to the tab you chose. A sheet or a run's
detail that was open closes. A shortcut only opens a screen. It never does
anything else for you.

## Put a shortcut on your home screen

1. Open the app and go to **Paired computers**.
2. Under **Home screen**, find the row for **Inbox** or **Messenger**.
3. Press **Add**. Android shows its own "Add to Home screen" dialog. Confirm it.

The new icon is named "Inbox" or "Messenger" and opens that tab. If your
launcher can't add shortcuts, **Paired computers** has no **Home screen** group.

## If you haven't paired yet

A shortcut opens **Pair with a computer**, with one more line under the intro:
"After pairing, Inbox opens." (or "After pairing, Messenger opens."). When you
finish pairing, the app opens on that tab.

## Back

From a tab you opened with a shortcut, **Back** leaves the app.
