# Open Inbox or Messenger from the Android app icon

On Android, a long press of the Halonic icon shows shortcuts that open a
screen of the app directly.

## Use the shortcuts

1. Long-press the Halonic icon on your home screen or in the app list.
2. Choose **Inbox** or **Messenger**.

The menu can also show your last chat as a third shortcut, named after that
chat. Choosing it opens that chat in Messenger. If Studio has no last chat, it
opens Messenger.

## Put a shortcut on your home screen

Android can also pin Inbox or Messenger as its own icon on your home screen.
Studio asks Android to do it, and Android shows its own dialog for you to
confirm.

## Good to know

- A shortcut only opens a screen. It never sends or changes anything.
- If your phone isn't paired with a computer yet, a shortcut opens the app as
  its icon does.
- Not every Android launcher shows long-press shortcuts.
