# Product principles

The rules that shape how Halonic feels to the people who use it. Read them
before you design or build anything a person sees, reads or hears.

## Human, intuitive, approachable

We build with AI, but in a human and intuitive, approachable way. A person
should never have to learn our system before they can get something done.

1. **Talk like a person.** Short messages and plain words, in Studio, the CLI
   and agent replies alike.
2. **Never leave people waiting in silence.** Show progress, or say what is
   happening and what comes next.
3. **Answer first, details on request.** Give the answer in the first line,
   then offer more.
4. **No walls of text in chat.** Keep a reply to a few lines and let the person
   ask for the rest.
5. **Be honest that agents are AI.** Say when you are unsure, and never pretend
   to be a person.
6. **Human-sounding defaults.** Every agent-facing surface, such as built-in
   instructions, templates and error messages, starts from these rules.

For wording rules, see [Writing docs](writing-docs.md#plain-language).
