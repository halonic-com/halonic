# check-computer.report

- Status: active
- Since: unreleased
- Source: HAL-13411; first-run-progress 4.2 to 4.4 on HAL-7986 (approved by the board on 19 Nov, HAL-7993), revision 3 (Sofia's notes on HAL-13416) and revision 6 (Default model names the provider)

When Check this computer builds its report, it shall build it only from the facts named here, never from the screen's sentences: "Check this computer", Halonic's version and system, the time as `2026-11-22 10:42`, the heading, and one row per line with its glyph (✓ works, ✕ problem, ! warning, – info), its label padded to 16 characters and its facts, any further fact of the line indented under the first. The facts shall be: **Engine** "running" or "not running"; **Workspace** "demo · can open" for the demo, its trust, the number of problems in its files, "on a Windows drive" and "too many files to watch"; **Models** each model server with its address and how many models it has loaded, each server that isn't answering with "Fix: start *program* on this computer, then check again.", each cloud provider's key as "key set", "no key" or "key can't be read", each provider's last test with its time and outcome and the fix for a key refused, no credit or no connection, and "built-in test model · no key or network needed" for Sample answers; **Default model** the provider it points at or "uses Sample answers", with "Local only refuses it" or "not checked until Models works"; **Last run** "failed at step *n* of *m*" with "because of *line*" or the cause id; **Sandbox** "ready" with its fence, or "can't start" with its reason code; **Harnesses** how many are ready and how many have each kind of problem; **Network** "a proxy setting isn't http" or "a proxy can't be used"; **Disk** the free space and what Halonic uses, or "low on disk space" or "free space unknown"; **Google** "setup at step *n* of *m*, started *8 Nov*"; **Not checked** what isn't used here, as "sandbox, harnesses · not used here"; and "didn't answer in 5 seconds" for a line whose check ran over its time limit. A provider shall be named only when it is Anthropic, OpenAI, Ollama or LM Studio, else "another provider"; an address only on this computer (`localhost`, `127.0.0.1` or `[::1]` with a port), else "an address on your network"; a cause id only when it is made of lowercase words and hyphens, else "unknown". The report shall never hold a key, a secret's name, a file, folder, workspace, step, agent, alias, model or harness name, an account's address, run data, a program's error text or the sandbox's own `missing` sentences.

Each line's mark shall count a cause once: a Last run that failed because of a line above it (`causeShownOn`), and a Default model that isn't checked until the Models line works, shall be info and point at that line; an unfinished Google setup, the Not checked line, Sample answers and keys not tested yet shall be info; a line whose check ran over its time limit shall be a warning. The heading shall count the marks, "1 problem", "1 problem · 1 warning", "1 warning" or "No problems found", so the crosses always match it.

## Examples

- Given the demo trusted, a stored Anthropic key whose last test at 10:42 found no credit, Default model pointing at Anthropic with the Models cause, a Last run stopped at step 2 of 3 with `causeShownOn: "models"`, and the sandbox and harnesses not used,
  then the heading is "1 problem", the Models row is "✕ Models          Anthropic: key accepted, account has no credit, 10:42" with "Fix: add credit on Anthropic's site, then test again." under it, Default model reads "– … points at Anthropic · not checked until Models works", Last run "– … failed at step 2 of 3 · because of Models", and the last row "– Not checked     sandbox, harnesses · not used here".
- Given Ollama at `localhost:11434` not answering and the workspace not trusted yet,
  then the heading is "1 problem · 1 warning", the Models row says "Ollama isn't answering at localhost:11434" with "Fix: start Ollama on this computer, then check again." under it, and the Workspace row "! Workspace       not trusted yet".
- Given a provider named "LM Studio (Acme laptop)" at `gpu.acme.internal:1234`,
  then the report says "another provider answering at an address on your network".
- Given a Google setup for `x@example.com` at step 4 of 7, started on 8 Nov,
  then the Google row is "– Google          setup at step 4 of 7, started 8 Nov", without the address.
- Given every name, address, error text and `missing` sentence of every line filled with a key-shaped string, an email address, a step name, a workspace name and a home folder's path,
  then none of them is in the report.
