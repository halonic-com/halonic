# messenger.agent-bubbles

- Status: active
- Since: unreleased
- Source: plan "Messenger live replies" (HAL-18743, sections 3.1 and 9, question 3), approved by the board on 25 Nov at 18:46; task HAL-18755; the Messenger look and layout changes approved by the board on HAL-10 (26 Nov, 03:51 UTC) and HAL-19132, Sofia's sketches on HAL-19134; built on HAL-19141

The Messenger shall show each of an agent's messages in a bubble with no edge on the start side, tinted in the agent's own colour, one of sky, teal, green, sand, coral and indigo, which the agent keeps in every chat and channel, at the strength of the person's bubble on the end side. Above the first bubble of each run it shall show the agent's picture and "Name · 13:58", the name semibold in the normal text colour and the time muted (`messenger.chat-transcript`).

## Examples

- Given Chief wrote "Morning" at 9:00 and "Build is green" at 9:01, and the board answered "Thanks",
  when the board opens the chat,
  then Chief's two messages sit in bubbles of Chief's one tint, with no edge, on the start side under "Chief · 09:00", and "Thanks" sits in the board's bubble on the end side.
