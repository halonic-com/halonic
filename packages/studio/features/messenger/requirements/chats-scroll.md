# messenger.chats-scroll

- Status: active
- Since: unreleased
- Source: the board's request on HAL-20556 (rows in the sidebar should scroll in the sidebar, with no window scrollbar)

When the chats don't fit the Messenger's sidebar, the rows shall scroll inside the sidebar, below the chat search and the Needs you entry, which shall stay in place. The list shall take the room the sidebar has left and no more, so a long list never makes the window scroll.

## Examples

- Given more chats than fit a 700px-high window,
  when the Messenger shows them,
  then the rows scroll inside the sidebar, and the search and Needs you stay at its top.
