# inbox.header-avatar

- Status: active
- Since: unreleased
- Source: the board's feedback on HAL-19536, item 3 (bigger avatars, especially in headers), plan approved by Liam on HAL-19539; the shared header of HAL-19192; built on HAL-19586

When an agent's or a person's item is open, the Inbox shall show the asker's picture at 40px before the line that says who asks and the item's title, as the Messenger's chat header does. The list's rows shall keep their 28px pictures.

## Examples

- Given a question from Chief, whose title is Chief of Staff,
  when you open it,
  then a 40px picture with "C" sits before "Chief · Chief of Staff asks" and the question's title.
- Given the same question,
  when the list shows it,
  then its row's picture stays 28px.
