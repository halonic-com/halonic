# inbox.one-count

- Status: active
- Since: unreleased
- Source: HAL-18557; design review HAL-18481, improvement 7 and flow 5.1 (approved by the board on HAL-10)

The Inbox shall count what waits for you once, the Inbox's own items and the agents' cards together, and show that one count both in the Needs you heading and in the open item's place ("2 of 6"), on the desktop as with one pane at a time. A bundle of an agent's cards shall count each card, and an item that no longer waits shall show no place.

## Examples

- Given two approvals and four cards from agents,
  when you open the first agent card,
  then the heading says "6 waiting" and the card's bar says "3 of 6".
