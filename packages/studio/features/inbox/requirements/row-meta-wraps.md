# inbox.row-meta-wraps

- Status: active
- Since: unreleased
- Source: HAL-15126 (C543 I3, Noah's decision)

The line under an item's title in Needs you, in the Inbox and on Home, shall wrap onto a second line and then be cut with an ellipsis, so a long line such as "Support reply · run 891b5a · a person step · Waiting 1 h" still shows how long the item has waited. Its words and their order shall stay as they are.

## Examples

- Given a run of Support reply waiting at a person step for an hour,
  when you open the Inbox,
  then its row's second line may take two lines and ends in "Waiting 1 h".
