# kit.rail-glass-token

- Status: active
- Since: unreleased
- Source: HAL-21048 (post-merge check HAL-21037 of #6915); the approved mockup, v26 on HAL-20617

The kit's theme shall have the selected rail drawing's fill, `--color-rail-glass`: the icons' deep blue glass, `#0a1630`, with the same value in light and in dark.

## Examples

- Given the dark theme,
  when the rail fills its selected drawing,
  then the fill is `#0a1630`, as in light.
