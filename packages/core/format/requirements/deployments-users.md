# format.deployments-users

- Status: active
- Since: unreleased
- Source: HAL-13212, HAL-16550, HAL-16577 (selling layer design on HAL-12483, sections 1, 2 and 8, slice S1)

When `parseFile` reads a `deployments.yaml`, the format shall accept on each deployment an optional `users` block with `mode` (`owner` or `many`, `owner` by default), `meter` (`none`, `config` or `cloud`, `none` by default), an optional `config` block with a list of `limits`, and an optional `cloud` block with a required `product`, and shall refuse any other key in these blocks. Each limit shall have a `unit` (`runs`, `steps`, `tokens`, `cost` or `harnessMinutes`), an `amount` above zero, a `period` (`day`, `month`, `run` or `step`) and an optional `action` (`stop` or `notify`, `stop` by default). `product` shall be 1 to 64 letters, digits, `_` or `-`. When `meter` is `cloud` and there is no `cloud` block, the format shall fail with `users-cloud-product-missing` at `meter`. When two limits share a unit and a period, it shall fail with `users-limit-repeated` at the second one. When a `config` or `cloud` block is there but `meter` names another meter, it shall warn with `users-meter-settings-unused` at that block, without failing the file. A deployment without a `users` block shall read as before, with no `users` key.

When `meter` is `config` and there is no `config` block, or neither `config.limits` nor any organisation holds a limit or a `concurrency`, the format shall warn with `users-config-limits-missing` (at `meter` without a `config` block, else at `config.limits`), without failing the file.

The `config` block shall accept an optional list of `organisations`. Each shall have an `id` matching `^org_[A-Za-z0-9]{16,40}$`, optional pool `limits` in the shape of a limit (none by default), an optional `concurrency` (a whole number above zero) and a list of `members`, and shall refuse any other key. Each member shall have an `actor` matching `^eu_[A-Za-z0-9]{16,40}$`, a `role` (`owner`, `admin` or `member`), optional `limits` and an optional `concurrency`, and shall refuse any other key. When an organisation has no member with role `owner`, the format shall fail with `users-organisation-owner-missing` at its `members`. When an organisation lists an `actor` twice, it shall fail with `users-member-repeated` at the second one. When two organisations share an `id`, it shall fail with `users-organisation-repeated` at the second one. When two of an organisation's pool limits, or two of one member's limits, share a unit and a period, it shall fail with `users-limit-repeated` at the second one. When a member's limit has no pool limit with the same `unit` and `period`, it shall fail with `users-member-limit-outside-pool` at that limit; when its `amount` is above that pool limit's, it shall fail with `users-member-limit-above-pool` at its `amount`. When a member's and its organisation's `concurrency` are both set and the member's is higher, it shall fail with `users-member-concurrency-above-organisation` at the member's `concurrency`. A `config` block without `organisations` shall read as before, with no `organisations` key.

Rationale: the block is how a deployed project turns the selling layer on (design, section 1). A limit has the shape of a plan's Limit in Halonic Cloud (design, section 2), so moving from `meter: config` to `meter: cloud` keeps the limits as written. The product id is not a secret; the deployment key never goes in the file. Two limits on the same unit and period would leave it unclear which one holds. Settings for another meter only warn, so switching meters changes only `meter:`, as with Cloudflare settings on another host. With no block nothing changes, so existing deployments keep today's behaviour. `meter: config` with nothing to count acts like `meter: none`, so it warns rather than staying silent. An organisation has the shape of an organisation in Halonic Cloud (design, section 2): its members share one pool, so a member's limit is a share of a pool limit and can't be larger than it, and without an owner nobody may manage the organisation.

## Examples

- Given a deployment without a `users` block, when it is parsed, then it has no `users` key.
- Given `users: {}`, when it is parsed, then it reads `{ mode: owner, meter: none }`.
- Given `mode: many`, `meter: config` and the limits `{ unit: cost, amount: 20, period: month }` and `{ unit: harnessMinutes, amount: 600, period: month, action: notify }`, when it is parsed, then they come back as written, the first with `action: stop`, and with no warnings.
- Given `meter: cloud` and `cloud: { product: prod_acme_code }`, when it is parsed, then it is accepted.
- Given `mode: everyone` or `meter: stripe`, when it is parsed, then it fails with `invalid_value` there; given `seats: 5`, it fails with `unrecognized_keys` at `users`.
- Given a limit with `unit: minutes`, `amount: 0` or `-1`, `period: week` or `action: warn`, when it is parsed, then it fails at that field of that limit.
- Given `product: prod acme`, a web address or 65 characters, when it is parsed, then it fails at `product` with a message that says which rule it breaks.
- Given `meter: cloud` with no `cloud` block, when it is parsed, then it fails with `users-cloud-product-missing` at `meter`: `Deployment "web" uses meter "cloud", so it needs users.cloud.product`.
- Given limits on cost per month, cost per day and again cost per month, when it is parsed, then it fails with `users-limit-repeated` at the third limit: `Deployment "web" already has a limit on cost per month`.
- Given `meter: none` with a `config` and a `cloud` block, when it is parsed, then it passes with two warnings `users-meter-settings-unused`, at `users.config` and at `users.cloud`.
- Given `meter: config` with no `config` block, when it is parsed, then it passes with the warning `users-config-limits-missing` at `meter`: `Deployment "web" uses meter "config" but has no limits, so it counts nothing, like meter "none"`; given `limits: []`, the warning is at `config.limits`.
- Given an organisation with a pool limit on cost per month, `concurrency: 4`, an owner and a member with a limit of 100 cost per month and `concurrency: 4`, when it is parsed, then it comes back as written, with no warnings.
- Given an organisation id `org_short`, a member `actor: eu_short`, `role: boss`, `concurrency: 0` or `1.5`, or an extra key, when it is parsed, then it fails at that field.
- Given an organisation whose only member is an `admin`, or with no members, when it is parsed, then it fails with `users-organisation-owner-missing` at its `members`: `Organisation "org_acme0000000000001" needs at least one member with role "owner"`.
- Given one organisation that lists the same `actor` twice, when it is parsed, then it fails with `users-member-repeated` at the second one; given the same person in two organisations, it passes.
- Given two organisations with the same `id`, when it is parsed, then it fails with `users-organisation-repeated` at the second `id`.
- Given pool limits on cost per month, cost per day and again cost per month, and a member with two limits on cost per month, when it is parsed, then it fails with `users-limit-repeated` at the third pool limit, `Organisation "org_acme0000000000001" already has a limit on cost per month`, and at the member's second limit, `Member "eu_anna000000000001" already has a limit on cost per month`.
- Given a pool limit of 100 cost per month and a member's limit of 5 cost per day, when it is parsed, then it fails with `users-member-limit-outside-pool` at that limit; given a member's limit of 120 cost per month, it fails with `users-member-limit-above-pool` at its `amount`.
- Given an organisation's `concurrency: 4` and a member's `concurrency: 5`, when it is parsed, then it fails with `users-member-concurrency-above-organisation`; without the organisation's `concurrency` it passes.
