# server.google-sign-in

- Status: active
- Since: 0.1.0
- Source: HAL-710 (build plan B5, D6); google setup flow 3.5 (step 7) and the routes doc, section 5.1

The server shall, behind the sign-in and the same-site check, answer `POST /api/google/setups/<accountRef>/sign-in` by starting a sign-in for the account and answering `{ authUrl }`, the address to open in the browser, and by saving on the record that a sign-in started. It shall send Google back to this server's own port, at `GET /api/google/callback`. It shall refuse with 409 `sign_in_needs_this_computer` a request that is not addressed to a loopback name, and with 409 `google_setup_incomplete` a setup without a client ID and secret. It shall, behind the same sign-in and same-site check, answer `DELETE /api/google/setups/<accountRef>/sign-in` (Cancel) by dropping the account's pending sign-in through the `dropSignIn` port, so a callback that still arrives is refused, by saving on the record that the sign-in is over, and by answering 204 whether or not a sign-in was pending. A ref that has no record shall answer 404 `google_setup_not_found`. The callback shall not need a session, because the browser Google sends back may hold none: the sign-in's own `state` is the proof, and the sign-in package refuses a `state` it didn't make. The callback shall finish the sign-in, save on the record how it ended (the sign-in and each service's result, or that the sign-in is over), tell the open setup item through the `signInEvents` port, and answer a page of fixed text for the outcome. The page, the request log and every thrown error shall hold no authorisation code, no `state`, no token and no secret, and the request log shall record the callback by path without its query string.

When a sign-in comes back for another account than the connector's, nothing shall be saved but a note on the record, `wrongAccountAt`, which holds a time and no address. The server shall keep the address Google confirmed only in memory, one per account, for ten minutes, and answer `GET /api/google/setups/<accountRef>/sign-in/wrong-account` with `{ signedInAs }`, behind the sign-in; it answers 404 `google_setup_not_found` for an unknown account and 404 `google_wrong_account_not_found` when there is no note. A new sign-in start, a successful sign-in, a change of the connector's address (`PATCH` with `{ kind: "address-changed" }`) and a disconnect shall forget the note, and the first three shall also clear `wrongAccountAt`. The address shall not be written to disk, to a log, to an error or to the callback page. After a restart or after ten minutes the record still says `wrongAccountAt` but the route answers 404, so the open item shall show the general "the sign-in didn't match this connector" text with Sign in again, without the other address. To use the other address, Studio changes the connector's address setting and starts a new sign-in; the server has no route that accepts the other account and keeps no token for it.

When a callback ends as a cancel, a refused client secret or a block by the organisation's admin, the server shall also note it on the record as `lastSignInEnded`, `{ kind, at }` with `kind` one of `cancelled`, `secretRefused` and `adminBlocked`, a fixed word and a time and never text from Google. A new sign-in start, a successful or wrong-account sign-in and any other way a sign-in ends shall clear it, and the setup answer shall carry it as `lastSignInEnded`, `null` when there is none.

Rationale: the callback is the one request that carries Google's authorisation code, and it comes from a browser that may not be signed in, so nothing in it may be echoed, logged or kept beyond the exchange.

## Examples

- Given a setup with a client ID and a saved secret on a loopback name, then a start answers an `authUrl` and the record has `signInStartedAt`.
- Given a callback that signs in, then the record has `lastSignInAt` and the service results, the item gets a `signedIn` event, and the page names no code or state.
- Given a callback Google sent with `error=access_denied`, then the page says nothing was saved and the record's sign-in is dropped.
- Given a started sign-in and a cancel, then the answer is 204, the port dropped the account's sign-in and the record's sign-in is over; a second cancel answers 204 too.
- Given a callback that signed in another account, then the record has `wrongAccountAt` and no address, and the read route answers that address until ten minutes pass, a new sign-in starts, a sign-in succeeds or the account is disconnected.
- Given a callback that ends with `error=access_denied`, `invalid_client` at the code exchange or `admin_policy_enforced`, then the record has `lastSignInEnded` with `cancelled`, `secretRefused` or `adminBlocked` and the time, until the next sign-in starts.
- Given a wrong-account note and a `PATCH` `{ kind: "address-changed" }`, then the record's `wrongAccountAt` is `null` and the read route answers 404.
- Given a request to start over a LAN name, then the answer is 409 `sign_in_needs_this_computer` and no sign-in is made.
